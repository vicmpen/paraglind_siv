⚠ Educational visualization only. The aerodynamic model is a simplified 2D approximation — real wing behaviour depends on class, loading, conditions and pilot input. 3D maneuvers (spirals, asymmetric collapses, spins, wing-overs) are projected into the pitch plane and supplemented with bank/yaw indicators in the overlay. Never attempt these maneuvers without certified instructor supervision over a dedicated SIV environment.
